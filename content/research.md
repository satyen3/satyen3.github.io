## Research
I work at the intersection of Poisson and Courant geometry, deformation
quantization, and the BV-BFV formalism. A question presently guiding me is how
a dimensional reduction of geometric targets can be promoted to an equivalence
of quantum field theories at the level of a homological equivalence that preserves observables,
boundary data, and cutting and gluing.

### Current direction: BV-BFV transfer from Courant to Poisson sigma models

For an integrable Lie bialgebroid $(A,A^*)$, [Cabrera and Cueca](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.07193) showed that the
three-dimensional Courant sigma model on a cylinder
$\Sigma\times I$ reduces, at the target and classical levels, to the
two-dimensional Poisson sigma model on $\Sigma$ with target the Poisson
groupoid $(G_A,\pi_G)$. The quantum statement remains: one must control
the observables, residual fields, propagators and BV measures, as well as the
BFV data carried by the boundary faces, edges, and corners.

My proposed PhD project is to formulate this reduction as a BV-BFV transfer
kernel. The aim is to construct, first in linear and Lie-bialgebra models and
then perturbatively in the nonlinear theory, a strong deformation retract

$$
p i = \mathrm{id}, \qquad
\mathrm{id}-i p = QK+KQ,
$$

where $p$ is interval BV pushforward, $i$ lifts effective Poisson-sigma-model
observables back to the Courant theory, and $K$ is the homotopy witnessing that
the eliminated interval modes carry no additional cohomological information.
Homological perturbation should transfer the Courant interaction to the
multiplicative Poisson structure on $G_A$ and simultaneously correct the
observable lift.

The boundary question is central. I want to understand
whether dimensional pushforward intertwines the two BFV operators and modified
quantum master equations, and whether it commutes with gluing up to a canonical
chain homotopy. Possible failures should be computable: bulk, face, edge, and
corner corrections are to be organized into a stratified anomaly complex. The
longer-term goal is a reusable transfer principle for BV-BFV theories, with
applications to Poisson-Lie models, relational symplectic groupoids, and
eventually the BV pushforwards that appear in first-order gravity.

### Previous work

My thesis work, carried out at ICTS-TIFR Bengaluru under Rukmini Dey, extends
the framework of Esposito–Nest–Schnitzer–Tsygan, who showed the existence of quantum moment
maps for Poisson manifolds via symmetry-adapted formality. I construct an explicit example
in the Poisson setting using the Cattaneo–Dherin–Felder formal
symplectic groupoid as the source of a globalized Fedosov-type star product.
The result is a quantum moment map for any Poisson manifold equipped with a
Hamiltonian Lie algebra action which admits an invariant connection.

Although the Fedosov construction is not a technical ingredient of the new
project, it trained me in the same homological pattern: replace a difficult
quotient by flatness in a larger complex, construct an explicit lift, perform
the operation there, and descend. The proposed work extends this viewpoint
from an algebra of observables to entire BV-BFV theories.

My broader interests include AKSZ theories, Lie algebroids and groupoids,
perturbative quantization on manifolds with boundary, relational symplectic
geometry, and derived and shifted symplectic geometry.
