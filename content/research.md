## Research
I work at the intersection of Poisson and Courant geometry, deformation
quantization, and the BV-BFV formalism. A question I'm thinking about is how
a dimensional reduction of geometric targets can be promoted to an homological (BV-BFV) equivalence of quantum field theories that preserves observables,
boundary data, and cutting and gluing.

### Previous work

My thesis work, carried out at ICTS-TIFR Bengaluru under Rukmini Dey, studied the frameworks of geometric and deformation quantization, primarily those of Berezin, Fedosov and Kontsevich formality, along with how they all trade between levels of generality and physical imterpretability. In addition, I also worked on constructing quantum moment maps for Poisson manifolds equipped with suitable connections, using Fedosov-type star products on their symplectic realizations (revised version in preparation).

### Interests 

My broader interests include AKSZ theories, Lie algebroids and groupoids,
perturbative quantization on manifolds with boundary, relational symplectic
geometry, and derived and shifted symplectic geometry.

I’d also like to explore homotopy type theory and the ideas it is based on: Grothendieck’s view of spaces as ∞-groupoids, the intuitionistic logic of Brouwer, and Lawvere’s categorical logic. I’m especially drawn to its cohesive extension, which builds on Lawvere’s mathematical reading of Hegel and lets differential geometry and field theory be formulated synthetically, which is a place where my mathematical and philosophical interests meet.
