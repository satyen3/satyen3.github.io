I have always found it odd, the way weather can affect my sensibilities. Before any thoughts have a chance to circle my mind, the clouds seem to penetrate through its interface with consciousness. As if indicating an upcoming storm; or perhaps death. Every civilization imagines its own apocalypse – a storm of destruction which sweeps away the corrupted appendages of its age, clearing the ground for fresh new ones. Does this knowledge bring any consolation? I would be reluctant to agree.

My end began the day I saw her. After a rather hearty lunch I was sitting by the meadow, letting the various juices do their thing. My senses had retired for the day, and my mind was basking in the motionless landscape in front of me. That is when suddenly, a jolt of motion shocked me conscious. Lying on the grass by some dark flowers, there she was, rubbing her tiny face with a discerning tenacity. As I tried to make out her silhouette among the surrounding grass, her beauty jumped at me with a certain totalizing force. In one fell swoop the urge to approach her rushed my body. She hadn't seen me yet, which was a good thing.

My body turned towards her, and before I could pretend to compose myself it started to move. I tried looking around to see if there might be any witnesses, but I failed. I could not leave her sight even for a moment, since I knew she could disappear just as quickly as she appeared. As I slowly started inching towards her, my mind began climbing a tall ladder of questions. Am I *supposed* to do this? I can perceive her presence, and yet I do not understand it. I knew this was the end, but my legs kept staggering ahead.

Why does this represent a contradiction? I had heard stories, I knew about this as much as anyone else. The elusive life cycle, my turn, then your turn. That is how it works. But if so, why all the smokescreen? Why does my mind need to be convinced of what my body has already decided? It tells me I am opportune. Not everyone is lucky enough to fulfill this inherent lack. Where did this lack emerge from? Had I crossed the limits of my agency?

It was getting increasingly certain I had. I was sure I could smell her, but I cannot describe what it felt like. I could say I was possessed, but that implies an external influence which did not exist. My only worry then was to capture her reaction the moment she noticed me. When it finally happened after what felt like millennia, she simply turned her head to the side, and looked at me with blank eyes. She had a kind of disinterested posture which felt inviting. Maybe I had a chance to turn back before, but now it was impossible. I knew she would not let me.

She was motionless. Her eyes followed my every movement, as if judging whether my approach was worthy of her attention, and then suddenly – something changed. She looked up at the sky, and then back at me. Her eyes again dipped in the same unwavering blankness. But this time, it represented something. I understood it right away - she *saw* me. I realized what all of it meant. The point of existence – it was right there in her tiny eyeballs.

All the dread had suddenly changed colors into something infinitely more vibrant. The questions that remained felt like a distant memory, piles of excess I had molted off back where I came from. I felt love. My body was doused in fragility, a feeling of euphoria that numbed all my organic skepticism. But in its essence it was about death. In being seen by her I felt like a part of the universe marching forward in time. I was a body trying to justify its particular shape, made to discover that its purpose was to condense back into the whole. I knew, only then, that I had lived.

There I was beyond my comprehension, making my way across the final few shrubs between me and my destiny. Her specific form had become elusive. I could barely see her anymore. But I kept walking. As I took my final few steps, she positioned herself to receive me. Her gaze was still latched onto my movements. I took some time to take in where fate had led me – an unremarkable patch of grass, one which looked eager to swallow my own now-corrupted appendages. She embraced me at last, and in a cold but loving gesture, bit my head off my neck.

It is an amazing sight when true desire meets reality. Alas, it could only have been the last thing I saw.
