---
title: "The walk back home after dropping off your partner: a poem"
date: 2026-09-03
draft: true
tags: ["poem"]
weight: 2
---

In the light of this evening with you I dissolve\
The neon reflects in every puddle we twirl around our feet\
Our final steps together beneath the rainy tree tunnels\
Your house approaches at the end of a dutiful languish

I hate to have to touch you a final time\
unfurling the basket of your fingers in mine\
Our whispering bodies shrinking beneath peering eyes\
I turn away, hungry as a button waiting to be pressed

A long wet alley sounding hurtfully quiet\
Dogs that sense fear\
Gravel, a pair of tracks

I meet the sun on his way back to your side\
and remember to watch out for the train\
while checking my phone for your text
