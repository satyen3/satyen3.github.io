---
title: "Nostalgia turns into grief"
date: 2026-08-05
draft: false
tags: ["poem"]
weight: 2
---

the difference between effort and\
the memory of it\
is nostalgia.

You arrived on a bright Sunday morning\
and wired yourself to my frozen sorrows\
as two cracked eggs on a skillet\
with nowhere to go but within

I swim in your belly with my plastic lamp\
and curse the flight of stairs that led me here\
The miles of track I had to lay\
just to catch a glimpse\
of sunshine on your cheeks

I try to make sense of the green and the red\
without a ground to stand on\
without planets sitting still\
for the stars to memorize us all

With every pump and beep of my heart\
I yearn to refuse this sinking weight\
why is life so timid, a flame so dull\
every word a threat to diffuse

and what do I have to hope for\
but an excruciating pus-filled tumor\
sprouting from the very mouth\
whose sullen sentries once belonged to you

and so I fly away\
far above the dazzling lights\
until my blood has frozen.
