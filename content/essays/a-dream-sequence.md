Why does the stormy shore taunt us? The place where water meets land is being drowned by water - how presumptuous. How shameful it must be to drown twice.

On 13th of December 2001 I turned into a flower. It felt lonely when the thought of turning my head crossed my mind. At least that meant I still had a mind. I joined the coalition which presided over the bog. It was the only community which could be compelled by the tiny consciousness of a flower. I became the leader on day 23. We enacted a new law - a pollinator tax which must be levied every 3rd day, and the proceeds would go towards bog restoration efforts. One of the many things I did not anticipate was bureaucracy, which I was later promised is strictly a cross species contamination whose burden we all must share. Nothing is emergent in the language of flowers, it does not resemble the image of the Tree. The Tree, who had been silent all this time, finally spoke up. But we could not listen. Except for the wilted flowers, for they had already accepted death.

In the beaming sun we played our visual tunes. Where there is heat there is vibrancy - said my closest closeted friend. Closeted from what? I asked. His response was a vicious melody beyond my posturial limits. I am going in circles, I thought. The burning fire heats the lumber, which burns hotter yet. Flowers cannot burn though, and neither can the sky. Except on the days that threaten to defy the night. I have learned that it reminds people of the punchline for a popular joke within certain circles.

I've seen enough to decide everything - said my friend. He must not be asked what it is, though, since that would make him forget. It is ironic, I know, but so is our conspicuous existence; just look at our circulatory system - what a joke. How can the mere brightness of colours fool anybody?

I sank in the water tumbler yesterday. I did not feel heavy, I was perfectly buoyant throughout. It was the hand of God which pushed me down. God is angry with me. I cannot deny His anger, so I ignored my pain and laughed in His face. I must have struck a nerve since He decided to redefine my sinking as drowning.

I continue my journey, but now as a man. A human being, a human with being. I feel the urge to be entertained. The specimen I was granted today was a living dead contraption meant to be a play on space and time (a car). I was in the car driving on a curving highway. I could not count the lanes, they kept dividing and subdividing with each tick of the machine. Occasionally, unsuspecting avenues would merge into the street when it promised them a perennial transfer of fresh new concepts. I could hear the sound of a paper cup being filled with tea. I felt a great conviction about the idea that it was tea - not any other beverage - only tea. It was so great that the walls started bombarding my senses with the contrary notion - the smell of roasted coffee beans with a hint of cinnamon filled the car. Without any warning, the car decided to go for a swim in the shallowest parts of this sloppy masquerade.
