Hello! I'm Satyen. I'm a mathematical physics graduate, currently based at ICTS-TIFR Bengaluru. I'm working on deformation quantization, formal/relational symplectic groupoids, and the BV-BFV/AKSZ formalism. Apart from math, my interests circle around questioning hidden assumptions: in the foundations of quantum mechanics, in the philosophy of science and mind, and in the border between perception and cognition. I also like to paint, and write the occasional poem.

*“How can I tell what I think till I see what I say?”* (E. M. Forster). \
This site is a recent undertaking in that spirit, a place where my thinking and work take objective form, and remain in progress.
